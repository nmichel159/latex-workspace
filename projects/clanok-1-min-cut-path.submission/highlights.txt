A cut-path is an edge set that contains both a u-v path and a u-v cut.
Deciding whether a cut-path with at most k edges exists is NP-complete.
In graphs of diameter two, a minimum cut-path has c(u,v) + d(u,v) - 1 edges.
The same formula holds in cactus graphs.
In sparse random graphs, a minimum cut plus a shortest path is nearly optimal.
