## Build

```powershell
.\scripts\build-project.ps1 -Project cv
.\scripts\package-project.ps1 -Project cv -KeepComments    # check before the folder is sent
```

What to know when editing:
- Custom macros are at the start of the file: `\cvsect`, `\cvrole`, `\cvproject`, `\cvaward`, `\cvedu`, `\cvlang`, `\cvtag` / `\cvtagmain`, `\cvtimeline`.
- Timeline: the months are approximate; `\tlnow` (today's date on the axis) is set by hand – move it on every edit (`year + (month − 1)/12`).
- `\fillin{…}` marks text to be filled in; none may remain in the finished CV.
- The document must stay at 2 pages; after every change check the page breaks and overfull lines in the PDF.
- Publications in the CV must agree with `knowledge/author.md` (currently two manuscripts "in preparation").
