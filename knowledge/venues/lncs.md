## Series rules (Springer, *Lecture Notes in Computer Science*)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type, publisher | conference proceedings book series; Springer (Springer Nature) |
| LaTeX | LaTeX2e Proceedings Template (`llncs`): [zip](https://cms-resources.apps.public.k8s.springernature.io/springer-cms/rest/v1/content/27851904/data/LaTeX2e%20Proceedings%20Template%20ZIP), also on Overleaf; class version TODO(verify) after download; CMR fonts as in the template |
| Citation style | the instructions name the "MathPhySci" style for computer-science proceedings; `splncs04.bst` ships with the template, TODO(verify) which the template documents; supply the `.bib` or `.bbl`; DOIs strongly encouraged |
| Structure rules | theorem-like numbering consecutive, without section prefix (Lemma 1, not Lemma 1.1); no self-defined environments; appendix placed before the references; ORCID in the author header; *Acknowledgments* and a mandatory **Disclosure of Interests** statement (third-level headings at the end) |
| Length | full papers usually 12-15 or more pages, short papers 6-11; papers under 4 pages go to the back matter; each conference sets its limit |
| Camera-ready | all source files, `.bib`/`.bbl`, figures, final PDF, licence-to-publish signed by hand by the corresponding author; short file names; line drawings at least 800 dpi |
| OA | organisers may buy gold OA for a volume, or authors choose Open Choice; proceedings are not covered by Springer Nature's institutional OA agreements |
| AI policy | the instructions point to Springer Nature's policy; the book policy forbids AI authorship and asks for disclosure of AI use beyond light language polishing. Conferences may add rules (SOFSEM). [submission.md](../writing/submission.md#11-policy-table-checked-2026-10-08) |
| Self-archiving | TODO(verify): Springer "Open Access Policies for Books" page not opened |
