## How to search

1. Keywords with synonyms and older names of the problem; the problem name in quotation marks.
2. Sources: DBLP, arXiv (cs.DS, cs.CC, cs.DM, math.CO, cs.NE, cs.AI, cs.LG), Google Scholar, zbMATH; for ML also OpenReview and the proceedings indexes (PMLR, ICLR, NeurIPS).
3. Backward and forward: the reference list of the closest paper and the papers citing it.
4. Log the search immediately, with the queries exactly as entered.
